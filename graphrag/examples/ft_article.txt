Federal Reserve chair Jerome Powell told reporters in Washington on Wednesday that the central bank would hold its benchmark interest rate steady at 5.25 percent. Powell cited stubborn services inflation and a resilient labour market as reasons to delay further cuts.

JPMorgan Chase Inc. acquired First Republic Bank in May 2023 following the lender's seizure by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation. The deal expanded JPMorgan's wealth management business and added roughly 84 billion dollars in deposits.

Apple Inc. reported quarterly revenue of 89.5 billion dollars, ahead of analyst expectations. Chief executive Tim Cook attributed the beat to record services revenue and continued strength in iPhone sales across emerging markets, particularly India.

The European Central Bank, led by president Christine Lagarde, kept its deposit rate at 4.0 percent. Lagarde warned that the eurozone economy remained fragile and that the ECB would not rush to ease policy until wage growth moderated.

Tesla Inc. delivered 484,000 vehicles in the third quarter, narrowly missing Wall Street forecasts. Chief executive Elon Musk reiterated that Tesla planned to begin production of its Cybertruck in late 2024 at its Austin, Texas gigafactory.
